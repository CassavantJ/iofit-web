Questions

Is I/O Fit free?

Yes. Workout logging, programs, templates, exercise history, tools, achievements and cloud sync are free. I/O Fit Pro is an optional upgrade that adds food tracking, progress reports, the recovery map and body measurements.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use I/O Fit as a guest and your data stays on your phone. Create an account whenever you want a backup or to use I/O Fit on another device; your guest data comes with you.

How do I cancel Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. On iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. On Android, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. More help

What happens to my data?

Your training data is yours. It's never sold and there are no ads. Read the privacy policy for the details.
